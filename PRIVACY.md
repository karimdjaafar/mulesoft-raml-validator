This policy covers the Claude plugin **jasmine-raml-validator** ("RAML Validator
by Jasmine Conseil") and the `raml-validator` command, published by Jasmine
Conseil Inc.

## What the plugin does with your data

- **Your RAML files stay on your machine** (or in your Claude session). The
  plugin reads the specification you give it and writes its reports next to the
  paths you choose. It sends **nothing** about your RAML, your reports or your
  environment to Jasmine Conseil or to anyone else.
- **No personal data is collected**, stored or shared. There is no account, no
  telemetry, no analytics and no tracking.
- **One network access only**: the first time it runs (or with
  `raml-validator setup`), the command downloads the open-source Python packages
  **PyYAML** and **Jinja2** from the Python Package Index (pypi.org) into a
  private environment inside the plugin folder. That request goes from your
  machine to pypi.org and carries no data about you or your specifications;
  pypi.org's own privacy policy applies to it.

## When you contact us

If you open a GitHub issue or write to kdjaafar@jasmineconseil.com, we receive
what you choose to send (for example a RAML excerpt to reproduce a bug). We use
it only to answer you and to improve the plugin, and we never share it. Remove
anything confidential before sending it.

## Inside Claude

When you use the plugin in Claude, your conversation is handled by Anthropic
under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy; the plugin adds no data flow
of its own.
